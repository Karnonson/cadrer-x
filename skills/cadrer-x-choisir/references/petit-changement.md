# A small change — the interview shrinks

Followed by `choisir` when its step 1 meets a small change.

The interview shrinks to what is unclear, one question at most. **Résultat** and **Problème** from
what they said; **Pour qui** and **Pourquoi maintenant** in one line each, from what they said or the
product's own; **Mesure** what they will see once it is done (« le bouton dit "Envoyer" »);
**Existant** `le produit lui-même`. Step 2 decides the short path.

Then back to choisir's step 1: write it back, and its **Save** after the yes.
